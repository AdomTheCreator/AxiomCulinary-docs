# Your first crop and recipe

## Grow a tomato

1. Open `/culinary`, then the crop or crafting guide. Obtain `tomato_seeds` through the configured crafting recipe, or ask an administrator for test items.
2. Right-click the **top face of farmland** with the seeds. Leave the space above free.
3. Keep the farmland moist using nearby water, or use a watering can when farming without a nearby source. For a new can, first right-click **source water** to fill it.
4. Look at your crop for growth and water feedback. Bone meal or compost adds 15% of the total growth time while the crop is still growing.
5. Right-click the mature crop to harvest. The default tomato plant regrows; other crops may return their seed and be removed.

Growth requires moisture, the configured light level and a valid support. Progress advances only while its chunk is loaded. Grapes and hops use the **underside of an overhead fence**, rather than the farmland planting interaction.

## Slice the tomato

1. Obtain and place `cutting_board`.
2. Open its recipe page in `/culinary recipes cutting_board` and insert the displayed initial ingredients with right-clicks.
3. Right-click with an empty main hand to start.
4. Use the knife for the requested manual cuts. Follow the station's current instructions rather than repeatedly inserting ingredients.
5. Collect the ready result with an empty main hand.

Recipes that need heat pause when their heat source is missing. Fermentation and other unheated work do not need a fire.

## Recover or move things

- **Sneak + right-click, empty main hand:** recover the last unstarted ingredient, or cancel the last workflow batch according to its current state.
- **Sneak + left-click, empty main hand:** dismantle your crop or station.
- **Change the floor:** break and replace the block below the station normally. The station and its contents stay in place, subject to normal terrain permissions.

The [station guide](Stations.md) explains parallel cooking, containers and recovery in more detail.
